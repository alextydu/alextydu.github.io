# WordPlay Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1st 2025
**Last updated:** July 31st 2026

## 1. Who we are

WordPlay is a virtual reality rehabilitation application developed by
Intelligent Interactive Internet of Things Lab at Duke University
(`534 Research Drive`, Durham, North Carolina, `27705`, United States).

WordPlay is used in two settings, and what we collect differs between them:

**Supervised research and clinical sessions.** A participant uses the app under the
supervision of a therapist or researcher, as part of an approved study. Motion recording is
enabled in these sessions.

**General use.** The app is used outside a study. Motion recording is disabled and no
recording files are produced.

This policy covers both. Where a practice applies only to research sessions, we say so.

For questions about this policy, contact `alex.du@duke.edu`.

## 2. What data we collect

### 2.1 Collected in all sessions

**Meta account identifier.** When the app starts it performs a platform entitlement check
through the Meta Quest platform. This returns your Meta (Oculus) user ID and username. We
use the numeric user ID as a stable identifier so that the app can tell players apart
within a session and reconnect you to the correct role if you disconnect.

**Session and networking information.** A short room code you create or enter, the role you
select (player or observer), and the network address of the device you connect from. This
is required to route multiplayer traffic between headsets and our game server.

**Play space measurements.** The app reads the boundary you have drawn in your Quest system
settings, and the floor height from your device's room scan. It uses these to place the
virtual play area so it fits your physical space. We read these measurements; we do not
receive images, photographs, or a 3D reconstruction of your room.

**Shared spatial anchors.** When two people play in the same physical room, the app uses
Meta's colocation service to share a spatial anchor so both headsets agree on where the game
is. The anchor is created and shared by Meta's platform service.

### 2.2 Collected only in research and clinical sessions, with consent

When motion recording is enabled, the app additionally records:

**Head motion.** The position and orientation of your headset, sampled approximately 30
times per second for the duration of the session.

**Hand motion.** If hand tracking is enabled on your device: wrist position and orientation,
the positions of your five fingertips on each hand, per-finger pinch strength, hand size,
and tracking confidence. Sampled at the same rate.

**Body motion from optional external sensors.** In some study protocols, wearable trackers
are attached to the limbs or torso. Their position and orientation are recorded on the study
computer. These sensors are separate hardware provided by the research team and are not part
of your Quest headset.

**Gameplay events.** Round start and end times, and which stage of the game was active when.

These recordings are written to a file identified by your Meta user ID, on a computer
controlled by the research team.

### 2.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect or record camera images or video from your headset, audio or voice
recordings, eye tracking or face tracking data, your Meta friends list or social graph,
payment information, or your precise geographic location.

We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not run advertising or analytics SDKs.

---

## 3. How we use your data

**To operate the app.** The account identifier, session information, play space
measurements, and spatial anchors exist to make the game work: to assign you a role, to
place the play area safely inside your boundary, to keep two headsets in the same room
spatially aligned, and to reconnect you if your connection drops.

**For clinical assessment.** In research and clinical sessions, motion recordings are used
by the supervising therapist or researcher to assess movement — how far a participant
travels, how quickly, how symmetrically, and how they reach and grasp. This is the purpose
of the study you have consented to.

**For research analysis and publication.** Motion recordings are analyzed to produce
measures such as movement path length, speed, and range of motion. Results may be published
in aggregate or de-identified form. 

**We do not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we
do not sell it or share it with data brokers.

## 4. Legal basis and research oversight

Research use of WordPlay is conducted under a protocol approved by the Duke University
Institutional Review Board, protocol number `2026-0308`. Participation is
voluntary and requires written informed consent obtained before any session.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal basis is your
consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), and for health-related data, your explicit consent
(Article 9(2)(a)). 

---

## 5. Who we share data with

**Meta Platforms.** The app runs on Meta Quest hardware and uses Meta platform services for
account verification, avatars, and shared spatial anchors. Meta's own handling of that data
is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy at https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/.

**The research team.** Motion recordings are accessible to the investigators and clinical
staff named in the IRB protocol.

**Nobody else.** We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or disclose it to third
parties, except where we are legally required to do so, or as separately described in your
consent form.

## 6. How to request deletion of your data

**You can request deletion of your data at any time, from any country, free of charge.**

Email `alex.du@duke.edu` with the subject line "WordPlay data deletion request". To
help us locate your records, include the approximate dates of your sessions and, if you know
it, your Meta account username. You do not need to give a reason.

We will confirm receipt within `7` business days and complete the deletion within
`7` days. We will never charge a fee for this.

If you are a research participant, you may also withdraw from the study at any time by
telling the study team, and withdrawal includes deletion of your recordings on the same
terms.

### When we may be unable to fulfil a request

There is one circumstance in which we cannot delete data, and we want to be clear about it
in advance:

Once a recording has been **de-identified and combined into an aggregate dataset or
published analysis**, the individual contribution can no longer be located or separated out,
because the link back to your identity has been deliberately and irreversibly destroyed.
That destruction is a privacy protection, not a way of retaining your data — but its
consequence is that aggregated results cannot be unmade.

This applies only to data already aggregated at the time we receive your request. Everything
still held in identifiable form will be deleted. If any of your data has already been
aggregated, we will tell you so when we respond.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the data we hold about you,
correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy in a portable
format, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of what came
before.

To exercise any of these, email `alex.du@duke.edu`. We apply these rights to all
users regardless of region.

If you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your national data protection
authority. If you are a California resident, we do not sell or share personal information as
those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising
your rights.

---

## 8. Children

WordPlay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from
them. Meta Quest headsets have their own minimum age requirements.

---

## 9. Security

Motion recordings are stored on access-controlled computers managed under Duke University's
information security requirements. Access is limited to research personnel named in the IRB
protocol.

Data travels between the headset and our servers over the local network or the internet.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data and will
notify affected users and the relevant authorities of a breach where required by law.

---

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Where changes are
significant and affect research participants, we will notify you and, where required,
re-obtain consent.
